How it works

Built for the partner who wants to help and doesn't know what "help" means.

Asking "what can I do?" hands the planning straight back to whoever is already carrying it. Answering that question, specifically and with a reason, is the whole product.

01

It measures discretionary time

Not who did more chores. Hours that are genuinely your own, once sleep, paid work, commuting and the housework are out.

02

Money is a separate question

Four ways to split: proportional, residual, equal remainder, equal. Hours are never priced.

03

One suggestion a week

With the reason attached. Not a list. Not a backlog. One.

04

Both of you answer privately

Two quick questions each week, and neither of you sees the other's answers. Where you differ, EvenUS shows the gap, without taking a side.

What it will never do.

Some of these get asked for. And if the picture stays bad for a couple of months, EvenUS stops suggesting things, says so once, points toward someone qualified, and goes quiet.

No streaks, badges, points or leaderboards
A streak punishes whoever had a hard week.
No nudge button
A reminder belongs to a task, and goes only to whoever owns it, and is never labelled with who set it.
No notification about what your partner didn't do
That notification does not exist here.
No public score, ever
Nothing is shareable, and there is no feed.
No reading your location, calendar or screen time
Surveillance between partners is the opposite of the point.
