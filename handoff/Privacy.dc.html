EvenUS is an app for two people who live together. It compares how money, time and household work are shared between you, and suggests one thing to try each week.

The short version

Your household's data is visible only to the two of you. Nobody at EvenUS can read your incomes, expenses, or chore history.
We never tell one partner what the other did or didn't do. There is no notification for that, and no setting to enable one.
Your weekly check-in answers are private from your partner, permanently and by design. They are enforced as private in the database rules, not just hidden in the app.
We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.
There is no analytics SDK in this app. No third-party tracker, no advertising identifier, no automatic screen recording.

What your partner can and cannot see

This deserves its own section, because it is the question most people actually have.

Your partner CAN see

Every shared expense and bill, every chore and completion, your name, your working hours, your income share, your financial obligations, the household's scores and history, and every change either of you makes to a setting that affects the score.

Your partner CANNOT see

Your weekly check-in answers, your individual mental-load answers before you have both answered, your password, or your notification settings. If you turn on "hide my exact income" they see the resulting percentage and not the figure itself.

What we will never send

A notification telling one partner what the other did or didn't do. That notification does not exist in the app and there is no setting to turn it on.

Reminders

You can set a reminder on a shared chore, and either partner can set one on any chore. The notification goes only to the person who owns that chore, and it never says who set it. The owner can switch any reminder off, immediately, and we do not tell the person who set it. Reminders are scheduled on your own device and are not sent through our servers.

Legal basis (UK/EU)

Contract: account data, household data, and everything needed to provide the service you asked for.
Legitimate interests: security, abuse prevention, and fixing crashes.
Consent: push notifications, and a profile photo if you add one. You can withdraw either at any time in the app or your device settings.

Where your data is held

On Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions) in the United States, region us-central1.

If you are in the UK or EU, this is a transfer outside your jurisdiction. It is covered by Google's Standard Contractual Clauses; see Google's Cloud Privacy Notice.

How long we keep it

While your account exists, we keep your household data so your history stays accurate. A fairness app that quietly changed last month's numbers would be worthless.
When you delete your account, your identity, your private check-ins, your own mental-load answers, and your recorded obligations are deleted immediately.
Shared household history is not deleted while your partner still uses it. Your name is replaced with "Former member". This is deliberate: erasing your side of a shared expense would silently rewrite every past split and score that the person you lived with still relies on.
If you were the last member, the entire household and everything in it is deleted.
Backups and server logs may persist for up to 30 days.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. It takes effect immediately and does not need your partner's permission.

You can also request deletion by emailing support@evenus.app from your registered address. We will action it within 30 days. Web instructions: evenus.app/delete-account.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, and the right to withdraw consent. In the app you can already export all your data (Profile → Export) and delete your account. For anything else, email support@evenus.app.

You have the right to complain to your data protection authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

EvenUS is for adults aged 18 and over. You may record details about children in your household (first name, birth date, living arrangements) because those change the household's workload and costs. Children do not have accounts and cannot use the app. We do not knowingly collect data directly from anyone under 18.

Payments

The app is currently free. If paid features are introduced, payment is handled by Apple or Google under their own terms. We never see or store your card details.

Changes

If we change this policy materially we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always reflects the current version.
