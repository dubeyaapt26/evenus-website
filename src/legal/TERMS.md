## 1. What EvenUS is

An app for two adults sharing a home. You each record what you earn, what you
spend, and what you do around the house. EvenUS compares those and offers **one
suggestion a week** for something you might swap.

You need an account each. One shared login will not work, and is not supported.

## 2. Who can use it

You must be **18 or over** and legally able to enter a contract. Do not create an
account for anyone else, or add someone to a household without their knowledge.

## 3. Your account

## 4. What EvenUS is *not* — please read this one

**EvenUS is not financial advice.** It does not tell you how to split your money,
what is fair, or what you should do. It describes what you have recorded and
suggests one thing to try. Every decision is yours.

**EvenUS is not legal advice.** Where you record child support, maintenance, or
other obligations, the app treats them as fixed facts that change your available
income. **It does not calculate what any obligation should be, never suggests
changing one, and is not a substitute for a solicitor or a court.**

**EvenUS is not therapy, counselling, or a relationship service.** It is a
measurement and suggestion tool. It cannot assess a relationship and does not
try to.

**If your relationship is in serious difficulty, or if you feel unsafe, please
speak to a qualified professional.** Data showing an imbalance can confirm
something painful, and confirmation is not the same as help.

**The numbers depend entirely on what you both enter.** Incomplete or inaccurate
entries produce incomplete or inaccurate results. Nothing is verified against a
bank, an employer, or any other source.

## 6. Your content

What you enter stays yours. You grant us only the permission needed to store it,
show it to you and your partner, and run the service. We do not sell it or use it
for advertising.

Both partners can see and use shared household data. **You cannot withdraw your
partner's access to the shared history of a household you both used** — see the
Privacy Policy on why deletion works the way it does.

## 7. Ending a household

Either of you can leave at any time, and neither needs the other's permission.

**Shared history stays with the household, not with whoever leaves.** Your name
becomes "Former member" and your private answers are deleted. If you were the
last member, everything is deleted.

We are not a party to anything between you and the person you shared a home
with, and we will not adjudicate a dispute between you or take a side. **We do
not provide data from your household as evidence in a dispute between you,
except where we are legally required to.** Either of you can export your own
copy at any time.
