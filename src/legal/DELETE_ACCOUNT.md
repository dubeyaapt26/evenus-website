# Deleting your EvenUS account

You can delete your account and your personal data at any time. **You don't need
your partner's permission, and they aren't asked to approve it.**

## In the app

**Profile → Delete account.** It takes effect immediately.

## By email

Email **[CONTACT EMAIL]** from the address you signed up with, asking for your
account to be deleted. We will action it within 30 days.

## What is deleted straight away

- Your login, name and email address
- Your weekly check-in answers, all of them
- Your mental-load answers
- Your recorded income and financial commitments

## What is kept, and why

- **Shared expenses, chores and bills** stay with the household, because they are
  also your partner's records. Your name is replaced with "Former member".
  Deleting them outright would silently rewrite every past split and score the
  person you lived with still relies on.
- **If you were the last member of the household**, everything is deleted.

There is no shadow copy, no thirty-day holding period, and no reactivation
window. Backups and server logs may persist for up to 30 days.

## Take a copy first

Before deleting, you can export everything of yours as a file:
**Profile → Export**.
