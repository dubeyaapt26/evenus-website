## The short version

EvenUS is an app for two people who live together. It compares how money, time
and household work are shared between you, and suggests one thing to try each
week.

- **Your household's data is visible only to the two of you.** Nobody at
  [COMPANY NAME] can read your incomes, expenses, or chore history.
- **We never tell one partner what the other did or didn't do.** There is no
  notification for that, and no setting to enable one.
- **Your weekly check-in answers are private from your partner**, permanently
  and by design. They are enforced as private in the database rules, not just
  hidden in the app.
- **We do not sell your data, and we do not show ads.**
- **There is no analytics SDK in this app.** No third-party tracker, no
  advertising identifier, no automatic screen recording.

## What we collect, and why

### You give us

| What | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email address | To create your account and connect you to your partner | You, your partner (name only) |
| Income and paid working hours | These are direct inputs to the fairness calculation — it cannot work without them | You and your partner. You can hide the exact figure so your partner sees only the resulting share |
| Expenses, bills, and how they're split | The money side of the calculation | Both partners |
| Chores, who owns them, and completions | The time and effort side | Both partners |
| Children's first names, birth dates, living arrangements, and who is financially responsible | Children change both the workload and the cost split. Blended families need this to produce a number that isn't wrong | Both partners |
| Financial obligations (child support, maintenance, loans, family support) | Money committed before this household sees it. Without this the app would wrongly show someone as contributing less | The amount and type are visible to both partners, because they change the fair share. **Only you can edit your own.** |
| Weekly check-in answers (how fair the week felt, how you were) | To compare what the app calculates against what you actually felt | **You only. Your partner can never read these,** and neither can we in any identifiable form |
| Mental-load domain answers | To find where you and your partner disagree about who carries what | Only the summary is shared, and only once you have both answered |
| Appreciation notes you write | To send to your partner | Both partners |
| Profile photo, if you add one | Display only | Both partners |

### Collected automatically

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Authentication tokens and session data | To keep you signed in |
| A push notification token, if you allow notifications | To deliver your weekly summary and reminders |
| Crash and error reports | To fix what breaks. **Amounts, email addresses, names and household identifiers are stripped out on your device before a report is sent** |
| Standard server logs from our hosting provider (Google Firebase), including IP address | Security and abuse prevention. Retained per Google's defaults |

### We do not collect

Location. Contacts. Calendar. Photos other than one you deliberately choose.
Browsing or app-usage history. Bank account or card details. Advertising
identifiers. There is no third-party analytics or advertising SDK in this app.

## What your partner can and cannot see

This deserves its own section, because it is the question most people actually
have.

**Your partner CAN see:** every shared expense and bill, every chore and
completion, your name, your working hours, your income share, your financial
obligations, the household's scores and history, and every change either of you
makes to a setting that affects the score.

**Your partner CANNOT see:** your weekly check-in answers, your individual
mental-load answers before you have both answered, your password, or your
notification settings. If you turn on "hide my exact income" they see the
resulting percentage and not the figure itself.

**What we will never send:** a notification telling one partner what the other
did or didn't do. That notification does not exist in the app and there is no
setting to turn it on.

## Reminders

You can set a reminder on a shared chore, and either partner can set one on any
chore. **The notification goes only to the person who owns that chore, and it
never says who set it.** The owner can switch any reminder off, immediately, and
we do not tell the person who set it. Reminders are scheduled on your own device
and are not sent through our servers.

## How long we keep it

- **While your account exists**, we keep your household data so your history
  stays accurate. A fairness app that quietly changed last month's numbers would
  be worthless.
- **When you delete your account**, your identity, your private check-ins, your
  own mental-load answers, and your recorded obligations are deleted
  immediately.
- **Shared household history is not deleted while your partner still uses it.**
  Your name is replaced with "Former member". This is deliberate: erasing your
  side of a shared expense would silently rewrite every past split and score
  that the person you lived with still relies on.
- **If you were the last member**, the entire household and everything in it is
  deleted.
- Backups and server logs may persist for up to 30 days.

## Children

EvenUS is for adults aged 18 and over. You may record details about children in
your household — first name, birth date, living arrangements — because those
change the household's workload and costs. **Children do not have accounts and
cannot use the app.** We do not knowingly collect data directly from anyone
under 18.
