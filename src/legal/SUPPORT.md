# Help with EvenUS

## Getting in touch

Email **[CONTACT EMAIL]**. One person reads it, usually within two working days.

Say which phone you are on and what you were doing when it went wrong — it saves
a round trip.

## The questions that come up most

### My partner hasn't appeared yet

EvenUS needs both of you. Until the second person joins, the household is there
but most screens have only half the picture.

Open **Invite** and send the code again, or share the link. The code does not
expire. If they have already signed up on their own, they can enter the code
from **Setup → Join a household** rather than creating a second household.

### We each created a household by mistake

Whoever has less in theirs should delete their account and join the other with
the code. **Profile → Delete account.** Nothing in the household you are joining
is affected.

### A reminder never arrived

Reminders are set on a chore, not on a person, and they go only to whoever owns
that chore. Check three things: the chore has an owner, the reminder is on, and
notifications are allowed for EvenUS in your phone's settings.

Everything else in the app works normally without notification permission.

### Why can't I see my partner's check-in answers?

Because they are private. Both of you answer the weekly questions separately and
neither of you sees the other's answers — the app uses them to work out where the
two of you see things differently, and shows that, without quoting anyone.

That is the design, not a fault.

### Can I edit something my partner logged?

No. Neither of you can change or remove the other's entries. An app where you
can correct each other's records becomes an argument with a database attached.

If something is genuinely wrong, the person who logged it can fix it.

### Deleting your account

**Profile → Delete account**, or see [Deleting your account](/delete-account/).
You do not need your partner's agreement and they are not asked to approve it.

## Reporting something that is wrong

If EvenUS has shown you something that reads as blame, or a suggestion that
landed badly, please send it to **[CONTACT EMAIL]** with a screenshot. That is
the failure this product cares most about and the reports are read individually.
