Complete Step-by-Step: How to Set Up EvenUS With Your Partner in Under 60 Seconds

Discover the best apps to fairly divide household chores with your partner or housemates. From smart apps to simple systems that actually work.

  • aaptd26@gmail.com
  • February 28, 2026
  • 4:11 am

You’ve both made the beautiful decision to create more fairness, peace, and love in your relationship. Congratulations — this one small step will change how you see and support each other every single day.

The best part? Setting up EvenUS is deliberately designed to be incredibly fast, simple, and stress-free. No long forms. No email verification. No complicated accounts or passwords. Just two smartphones and a few gentle taps — and you’ll be fully set up in under 60 seconds.

Here is the complete, detailed, step-by-step guide that thousands of couples have already followed successfully. We’ve also included extra tips, what to expect on each screen, and what happens right after setup so you feel completely confident.

Step 1: Download the App on Both Phones (10–12 seconds) One of you opens the App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android) and simply searches “EvenUS”. Tap the official EvenUS app (it has the soft green heart logo) and hit “Get” or “Install”. While it downloads (usually 8–10 seconds), the other partner does the exact same thing on their phone.

Pro tip: Do this together while sitting on the couch — it turns the setup into a fun, shared moment instead of a task.

Step 2: Open the App and Choose “Start New Relationship” (8–10 seconds) Open EvenUS on the first phone. You’ll see a warm, welcoming screen with the tagline “No More Money Fights. Just Fairness & Love.” Tap the big, friendly button that says “Start New Relationship”.

The app will ask you to choose “I am Partner A” or “I am Partner B”. It genuinely doesn’t matter who picks first — you can rename yourselves later to your real names (like “Priya” and “Arjun”). This step is purely for pairing.

Step 3: Generate and Share the Secure Pairing Code (12–15 seconds) The app instantly creates a unique 6-digit secure code that appears large and clearly on the screen. Simply show the code to your partner or read it out loud.

On the second phone, your partner taps “Join Existing Relationship” and types in the exact same 6-digit code.

As soon as the code is accepted, you’ll both see a beautiful “Connected!” animation with hearts. Your two phones are now securely paired using end-to-end encryption. Your data is completely private — visible only to the two of you, never stored on public servers, and never shared with anyone else.

This pairing process is faster and safer than most banking apps.

Step 4: Add Basic Information (10–12 seconds) The app now gently asks for two simple things:

  • Your monthly take-home incomes (this powers the income-aware splits — you can change it anytime)
  • Whether you’d like gentle reminders turned on by default (highly recommended — you can adjust or turn them off later)

You can skip any field and fill it in later. The app is designed to feel supportive, never pushy.

Step 5: Welcome to Your Live Dashboard (5–8 seconds) The screen transitions to your beautiful Live Household Balance Dashboard. You’ll see your starting balance (usually around 50% until you add your first tasks) and the clean Activity Feed ready for your first entries. Both phones now show exactly the same real-time view.

Total time from opening the app stores to seeing your dashboard: under 60 seconds.

What Happens Immediately After Setup

  • Both phones are perfectly synced in real time
  • You can immediately start adding your first shared expense (e.g., this month’s rent) to see the income-aware split work instantly
  • The app sends both of you a warm welcome message with a quick tip
  • Your first Weekly Fairness Report will arrive next Sunday (gentle, positive, and helpful)
  • All data is automatically backed up securely

Quick Tips to Make Your First 5 Minutes Even Better

  • Rename yourselves right away (tap the profile icons) so it feels personal
  • Add your first shared expense together — watching the fair split calculate live is magical
  • Turn on notifications for both phones (they are soft and never annoying)
  • Explore the demo mode if you want to play around before adding real data

Why This Setup Feels So Different (and So Good)

Most apps make you feel like you’re doing administrative work. EvenUS makes you feel like you’re taking care of your relationship together. The entire process was designed by couples for couples — fast enough for busy lives, warm enough to feel like love.

You’re now part of a growing community of couples who have moved from arguments to 90%+ harmony in weeks.
