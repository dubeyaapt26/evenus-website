Why couples look beyond Tody

Tody is a well-liked cleaning app: 4.8 from 11K ratings on the US App Store when we checked. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Android (there is no web version), and it gives every task its own realistic frequency and shows what is due, which its App Store listing describes as managing cleaning by indicators of actual need rather than arbitrary dates. Assigning or rotating chores is free on every plan, and its FairShare feature turns an agreed split into a monthly target for each person.

The catch for couples is sharing. Tody's free plan is the core cleaning plan for one person. For both of you to see the same live plan on your own phones you need Premium+, which on Tody's site is $25 a year for Duo (2 members), $40 for Family and $80 for Team, with a 30-day free trial. Couples who look elsewhere usually want sharing for free, want gamification of a different kind, or want an app that handles more than cleaning.

1. Sweepy: closest to Tody

Best for: couples who want a cleaning schedule with a bit of competition.

Sweepy invites family members or roommates to share the cleaning, gives each task up to 3 points, and has a family leaderboard. It runs on iPhone and Apple Watch and has an Android app. You can create as many custom tasks as you like for free; its site says a generated everyday checklist is a premium feature. Neither its site nor its App Store listing says whether inviting your partner needs premium, and Sweepy's site prints no prices, so check the App Store listing. One thing to know: its iPhone app had not been updated since February 2026 when we checked.

For a side-by-side, see Tody vs Sweepy, and Sweepy alternatives if it is not quite right either.

2. Nipto: best free sharing

Best for: couples who want both phones on the same chore list without paying.

Nipto is built as a game for you and your partner, your family or your roommates. Each task earns 5 to 100 "eggs" depending on the effort, and Nipto rotates a task between you based on who did it last. Inviting someone is not a Premium feature, and the free plan includes 5 schedules; Premium adds unlimited schedules, stats and other extras, with one purchase covering everyone. It works on iPhone, Android or in a browser. Nipto's site does not print the Premium price.

3. Chap: best plain shared chore list

Best for: couples who want a simple list with names on it, not a game.

Chap says it is for couples, roommates and families. The free plan includes unlimited members and assigning chores to anyone. Rotations, nudges and longer history are Premium, which is $39.99 a year on both its site and the App Store. It runs on iPhone and Android.

4. Flatastic: best for chores plus shopping and costs

Best for: couples who want one app for the cleaning plan, the grocery list and who owes whom.

Flatastic names couples alongside flatmates and families. It has a chore rotation with a score system, a shared shopping list, an expense tracker and household notes, on iOS, Android and a web app. Its Premium page lists chore history, notes on chores and no ads as the chore extras, which suggests shared chores are on the free plan, though it does not say so in those words. Its site gives no prices. See Flatastic alternatives for more.

5. Spotless: best calm cleaning view

Best for: couples who want to see at a glance what needs cleaning, without points or rankings.

Spotless shows how much each task is in need of cleaning and syncs rooms and tasks across everyone's devices. Its site says household members join free but the household needs a subscription. It is on iPhone and iPad; its site has no Android link. No prices are printed on its site.

6. Cozi: best if you also need a family calendar

Best for: families who want chores in the same place as the calendar and shopping list.

Cozi is a family organizer, free for every family member on every device, with Gold at $39 a year. Its site describes a new Cozi Chores option for repeating tasks and lets you assign family members to a to-do or chores list. It is not a cleaning scheduler like Tody, but it saves a second app. See Cozi alternatives if you are weighing organizers.

Where EvenUS fits

Best for: couples where the argument is not "what needs cleaning?" but "who is doing it?".

EvenUS is our app. Chores is one shared housework list with an owner on each chore, and each tick records the minutes the chore really took. Chore reminders go only to the chore's owner and never say who set them. The time you log becomes part of a comparison of each partner's free hours in a week, alongside paid work, travel and children, and EvenUS suggests one swap a week to try.

Its limits: there are no points, streaks or leaderboards, it is built to show how housework is shared rather than to plan a cleaning routine, and it only works for two people. It also handles shared costs, which Tody does not, but it never connects to a bank. It is in closed testing, so it is not on the app stores yet. One subscription covers both of you: a 7-day free trial with no card, then $9.99 a month or $79.99 a year (USD reference prices, set per region by the app stores).
