Cozi and FamilyWall are both family organisers, and the Terms of each say the two websites are operated by the same three companies: Cozi, Inc., Family & Co. and In Tandem Families. Cozi is the simpler one: a colour-coded family calendar, shared lists and meal planning, free with ads, or $39 a year for Cozi Gold. FamilyWall is the broader one, adding a messenger, photo gallery and, in Premium, a budget tracker and location alerts, for $44.99 a year (prices checked 29 October 2026).

  • Choose Cozi if your family mostly needs one shared calendar, a shopping list and a meal plan that everyone can see, and you like a daily or weekly agenda by email.
  • Choose FamilyWall if you want calendar, lists, family chat, photos and location in one app, or you organise more than one group, since one Premium subscription applies to the first five circles you create.
  • Choose EvenUS if the calendar is fine but the two adults disagree about who carries more of the work, the planning and the costs.

What Cozi does well

Cozi's core is free on iPhone, Android and the web, and one account holds the whole family, so nobody has to buy their own copy, and the Gold features page says the ad-free experience applies to everyone in the family. On 29 October 2026 it was rated 4.8 on the US App Store (about 402,000 ratings), with more than 5 million Google Play downloads. The calendar is the part most families come for. Each person gets a colour, you can set reminders for yourself or for someone else, and Cozi can send a daily or weekly agenda by email, which suits a parent who plans the week from an inbox. It also works with Google, Outlook and Apple calendars, so you can subscribe to a school or club calendar rather than typing it in.

The food side joins up neatly. Recipes sit in a recipe box, their ingredients can go straight onto the shopping list, and meals can be scheduled onto the calendar. In April 2025 Cozi added a Chores list, where recurring chores are assigned and tracked per family member and each person has their own list, on iOS and Android.

Cozi Gold, at $39 a year (checked 29 October 2026), removes the ads and adds a month view on mobile, calendar search, up to three reminders per event, change notifications, a birthday tracker, Shopping Mode and VIP support. Cozi Max, at $79 a year on cozi.com ($79.99 on the App Store), adds AI tools: event import, a recipe creator and a meal planner. The App Store listing also shows several Cozi Gold prices between $29.99 and $59.99 alongside the $39 one.

Where Cozi is limited

The free version shows ads. Cozi's App Store and Google Play listings put the ability to add, edit and view events more than 30 days ahead among the Gold features, which means free accounts do not have it; that limit is in the store listings rather than on the compare-plans page. There is no messenger, gallery or location sharing described on Cozi's pages, and the Family Journal was removed in 2021, with users asked to download theirs before 15 August 2021. Nothing on Cozi's official pages describes money tracking, splitting costs, time tracking or a view of how the work is divided between adults.

What FamilyWall does well

FamilyWall tries to be the whole family's phone hub. The App Store listing's free features are a shared colour-coded calendar with reminders, shopping lists, to-do and chore lists that can be assigned to a member, recipes, a messenger, a photo and video gallery and contacts; the premium page on familywall.com puts a Recipe Box in Premium only. It runs on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web, and on 29 October 2026 it was rated 4.8 on the US App Store (about 25,000 ratings), with more than 5 million Google Play downloads.

Premium is a large bundle for $44.99 a year (checked 29 October 2026), with a 30-day free trial. It adds a Budget Tracker with spending limits by category that can be shared with the family, a Meal Planner that feeds the shopping list, Documents, Timetables, sync with Google and Outlook calendars plus subscriptions to calendars by URL, real-time location with place alerts, audio and video messages, 25 GB of storage, a Quick Grocery List, a Recipe Box and no ads. One subscription covers every member of a circle, and the App Store listing says Premium applies to the first five circles you create, which helps if you also organise grandparents, a co-parent or a sports team.

Where FamilyWall is limited

The premium page lists ad-free use as a Premium feature and the Terms say users may be shown ads, so expect ads on the free version (the about page, confusingly, calls the app ad-free). Google and Outlook calendar sync is a Premium feature. The monthly price is not the same everywhere: $4.99 on familywall.com and the Play description, $7.99 on the US App Store list, so check the store you will pay through. The Budget Tracker records shared spending against limits; it does not work out who pays whom back. Nothing on FamilyWall's pages describes balancing work between two adults, time tracking or the mental load.

Price: what you pay for each

Both are free to start and both describe ads on the free version. For a year without ads, Cozi Gold costs $39 and FamilyWall Premium $44.99, a gap of under $6 (checked 29 October 2026). The bigger difference is what the money buys. Cozi Gold mostly improves the calendar: month view, search, extra reminders, change alerts. FamilyWall Premium adds whole areas, including budgets, documents, timetables, location alerts and calendar sync. Cozi Max at $79 a year is the option if you want AI help importing events and planning meals. FamilyWall lets you try Premium for 30 days; Cozi's pages we read did not state a Gold trial length.

Calendar: the part most families use daily

Both calendars are colour-coded and shared. Cozi's strengths are the agenda emails and subscribing to outside calendars, both listed among the free features on the store listings, with month view and search added in Gold. The 30-day limit on free accounts matters if you book holidays or appointments far ahead. FamilyWall's calendar shows one member or the whole family. Its Google Play listing puts importing your existing calendars among the free features, while its help centre marks the Google and Outlook import as Premium; Google and Outlook sync is listed under Premium on every page we read. If one parent lives in Outlook at work, that sync may decide it.

Lists, chores and meals

Here the two are close. Both have shared shopping and to-do lists, both let you give a chore to a named person, and both connect recipes to the shopping list. Cozi's recipe to list to calendar flow is part of its long-standing core, and its per-member Chores list arrived in 2025; FamilyWall's Meal Planner is Premium. Neither app records how long a chore takes or who did the noticing and planning before it.

Money and sharing the work between two adults

FamilyWall is the only one of the two with a money feature: the Premium Budget Tracker, which logs expenses and sets spending limits by category. Cozi's pages describe no money features. Neither app splits costs between partners by income, tracks each adult's free time or measures the mental load. Both assign tasks to people, which shows what is on each list, but not whether the overall load is shared the way either of you would choose. If that is the real question in your house, a list of tasks will not answer it, and our guide to what the mental load is explains why.

Which should you choose?

You mostly need everyone on one calendar

Cozi does this simply, with colours per person and agenda emails. If you plan more than a month ahead, budget for Gold. Pick: Cozi.

You want chat, photos and location in the same app

Of the two, only FamilyWall describes a messenger, gallery and locator alongside the calendar, with real-time location and place alerts in Premium. Pick: FamilyWall.

You run more than one group

FamilyWall's circles let one Premium subscription apply to the first five groups you create, such as your household and grandparents. Cozi is built around one family account. Pick: FamilyWall.

The calendar works, but the two of you disagree about who carries more

Neither organiser measures that. Try our free mental load quiz together first, then look at a fairness app. Pick: EvenUS, alongside whichever calendar you already use.

Common questions

Is Cozi free in 2026?

Yes. Cozi has a free plan with ads on iPhone, Android and the web. Its store listings say free accounts cannot add, edit or view events more than 30 days ahead; Cozi Gold at $39 a year removes the ads (checked 29 October 2026).

Which is cheaper, Cozi or FamilyWall?

Both are free with ads. Paid, Cozi Gold is $39 a year and FamilyWall Premium is $44.99 a year, both checked on 29 October 2026. FamilyWall's monthly price differs by store, so check the one you will pay through.

Are Cozi and FamilyWall the same company?

Their Terms point to the same operators. Both Terms pages say cozi.com and familywall.com are operated by Cozi, Inc., Family & Co. and In Tandem Families, and that the Terms are between you and In Tandem only; FamilyWall's Terms add that In Tandem Families operates the FamilyWall app. Cozi's privacy policy separately says Cozi is offered by OurFamilyWizard, LLC. The apps remain separate products with their own plans and prices.

Does FamilyWall work on Android?

Yes. FamilyWall runs on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web at familywall.com. Cozi also runs on iPhone, Android and the web.

Which has a budget or money feature?

FamilyWall Premium includes a Budget Tracker for expenses and spending limits by category, shared with family. Cozi's official pages describe no money features. Neither splits costs between partners by income.

Can Cozi or FamilyWall show who does more at home?

Both let you assign chores and events to a named person. Nothing on either app's official pages describes time tracking, balancing work between two adults or measuring the mental load.
