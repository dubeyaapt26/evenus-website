To set up a chore reminder in EvenUS, make sure the chore has an owner, open its reminder settings, choose a time and (optionally) the days, and save. The reminder goes only to the chore's owner, never says who set it, and the owner can switch it off at any time. You cannot use it to remind your partner, and that is deliberate.

Below are the steps, what the settings actually do, and how to tune reminders so they take remembering off you rather than adding noise.

What do you need before setting a reminder?

A chore with an owner, and notifications allowed for EvenUS on the owner's phone.

A reminder is part of a chore, not a message between people, so it needs someone to reach. If a chore has no owner yet, the reminder screen tells you there is no one to remind and asks you to give it an owner first. If you have not added chores yet, how to add chores, bills and mental-load tasks covers that, including the planning tasks people tend to leave off.

Reminders are scheduled on the owner's own device, so the phone has to allow notifications from the app. If they are switched off, the reminder screen says so plainly, because a reminder that never arrives is worse than none.

How do you turn on a chore reminder, step by step?

Five steps, about a minute in total.

  1. Open the chore from your chores list and go to its reminder settings.
  2. Read the line at the top. If it is your chore it says only you get this, and it is a note to yourself, not a message. If it is your partner's, it says only they get it, it will not say who set it, and they can switch it off whenever they like.
  3. Pick a time. The presets are 8:00 am, 12:00 pm, 5:00 pm, 6:00 pm and 8:00 pm. If you turn one on without choosing, it defaults to 6 pm.
  4. Pick days, or leave them blank. Blank means it only comes up when the chore is actually about due. Choosing days narrows that down further.
  5. Check the summary and save. The screen shows a plain description of what you are setting, such as "Every Tuesday at 8:00 pm", before you press "Set the reminder".

To change it later, open the same screen and press "Save". To delete it, press "Remove it".

What does the reminder actually say?

The chore's name and when it is on the list. Nothing about who set it and nothing about anyone waiting.

An evening reminder for the bins reads, in effect, "Bins: on the list for this evening." A morning one says "this morning", and a midday one says "today". Tapping it opens the chore.

How do you mute or turn off a reminder?

If you own the chore, open it and press "Turn this off". Nobody is told. Press "Turn it back on" to undo it.

Muting is one-sided by design. Asking your partner's permission to stop being reminded would turn a preference into a negotiation, which is exactly the conversation reminders are meant to remove.

To switch off chore reminders for everything you own, open Us, then Preferences, and turn off "Chore reminders". Its description says what it does: only for chores you own, and it never says who set the time. The same screen has separate switches for the weekly summary and your own weekly check-in reminder.

Can you set a reminder for your partner?

You can put a time on a shared chore that your partner owns, but you cannot send them a reminder. The difference matters.

Putting a time on a chore is like writing bin day on the kitchen calendar: it is admin. The owner receives it as the household's schedule, not as a message from you, and they can switch it off without telling anyone. There is no "remind them now" button, no repeat until it is done, and no notification to you if it is ignored or muted.

Older posts on this site said otherwise. They were wrong. The reasoning for the design is in chore reminders without nagging your partner.

How do you customise reminders so they help, not annoy?

Set them for the moment the owner would actually act, use fewer of them, and leave flexible chores without fixed days.

Match the time to the person's real evening

Take Priya and Tom. Tom owns the bins and gets home around 7 pm. A 5 pm reminder arrives while he is on the train and is forgotten by the time he is through the door. An 8 pm reminder on Tuesdays lands when he can act on it. Ask the owner, not the person who used to remember.

Use reminders for planning tasks as well as physical ones

The most useful reminders are often on mental-load chores: checking the school newsletter, renewing a prescription, ordering the weekly food shop. These are the tasks someone used to carry in their head. Moving the remembering onto the chore reduces the mental load in a way that moving the doing alone does not. What the mental load is explains why that remembering counts as work.

Leave days blank for chores without a fixed day

Changing the sheets or cleaning the bathroom does not need to happen on a set day. Leave the days empty and the reminder only appears when the chore is about due, so it does not repeat on days it is not needed.

Keep the total low

Even with the two-a-day cap, ten chores with reminders makes a phone feel like a to-do list with opinions. Start with two or three that genuinely slip, and add more only if they help.

What about bill reminders?

Bill reminders go only to the person who has to act, two days before the bill is due, and never show the amount.

They reach whoever manages the bill, plus whoever pays it if it is not on autopay. Bills on autopay produce no reminder at all. Amounts are left off so that a lock screen does not become a live feed of household spending. The chore reminder switch does not silence bill reminders, because they are not about chores. Common questions

Why did I not get a reminder I set?

Check that you own the chore, that notifications are allowed for EvenUS on your phone, that the time is not in quiet hours, and that the chore was not already ticked off.

Does my partner see that I muted their reminder?

No. Muting is silent, and nobody is told when a reminder is switched off or ignored.

Can reminders repeat until a chore is done?

No. A chore reminds at most once per cycle, and there is no escalation or repeat.

Do reminders affect the fairness score?

No. Reminders only help with remembering. The score comes from what is logged, as explained in how the EvenUS fairness score works.
